FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing

How do I get paid?

We work with multiple factoring companies for quick payments. You can choose weekly or bi-weekly payouts. Most drivers receive payment within 24-48 hours after delivery.

Are there any hidden fees?

No! Our pricing is 100% transparent. You only pay the percentage on loads we successfully book for you. No setup fees, no cancellation fees, no surprises.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! No long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime with zero penalties. For weekly plan, we just need 7 days notice.

What types of trucks do you work with?

We work with all types: Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Hot Shot, Power Only, and Box Trucks. Each type has different rate structures and we optimize accordingly.

Do you guarantee loads?

For Weekly Plan customers, we guarantee a minimum of 5 loads per week. If we don't deliver, you don't pay the weekly fee for that week.

How do I switch plans?

You can upgrade or downgrade anytime. Just contact your dispatcher and we'll adjust your plan from the next billing cycle.

100% Satisfaction Guarantee

If you're not satisfied with our service in the first 30 days, we'll refund your fees. No questions asked.

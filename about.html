About Us

Your trusted partner in professional truck dispatching since 2020

Our Journey

Driving Success for 500+ Truckers

Founded in 2020, Truck Dispatch Pro started with a simple mission: help owner-operators and small fleets maximize their revenue through professional dispatching services. What began as a two-person team has now grown into a trusted name in the trucking industry.

We understand the challenges truck drivers face daily - from finding quality loads to negotiating fair rates and handling paperwork. That's why we've built a system that takes care of everything, allowing you to focus on what you do best: driving safely.
